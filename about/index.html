About

Lackey Law Firm

The Lackey Law Firm has been serving all of North Alabama for over 40 years. As a family of lawyers, we always offer aggressive representation and reasonable prices. Every client is just as important as another, regardless of their case. You can get that same personal service on your case by calling 256-259-3929 now.

Frequently Asked Questions

See below for more informaiton about our practice.

 
Do you do free consultations?

Yes, the initial in-office consultation is always free and if you’re injured and can’t come to us,  we’ll come to you.

What kinds of cases do you handle?

Our focus is on car wrecks, personal injury and criminal defense, but we may handle your case regardless of the type, depending on all the circumstances.

What are your fees?

If you have an injury case, you pay us only if we recover money for you. For other types of cases, the fees depend on the circumstances but initial consultations are free.

Do you accept credit cards?

The Lackey Law Firm gladly accepts checks, cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, and debit cards.

Who will be working on my case?

Our attorneys each handle their own cases, but we also work together on many cases.

How should I contact you?

You can call us at 256-259-3929 or you can contact us by email at info@lackeylawfirm.com
