Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to talk to a lawyer?

Nothing. The first consultation is free, and for accepted personal injury cases you pay no attorney's fee unless we recover money for you. Your written fee agreement explains how case expenses are handled.

How long do I have to file an injury claim in Alabama?

Many Alabama personal injury claims must be filed within two years, but some deadlines are shorter. Contact us as soon as you can so we can check the deadline for your situation and preserve evidence.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?

It's best to speak with a lawyer before giving a recorded statement. Insurers may use what you say to reduce or deny your claim.

Where are your offices?

We have offices in Huntsville and Scottsboro and help clients throughout North Alabama, including Madison, Decatur and Athens.
