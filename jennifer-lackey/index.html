Jennifer Lackey

Jennifer Lackey

Jennifer graduated from the University of Alabama School of Law in 2011 and earned her B.S. in Criminal Justice from Faulkner University in 2008. Jennifer grew up in North Alabama and graduated from Scottsboro High School in 2000.

Jennifer is following in the footsteps of her father, Gary W. Lackey, being a strong advocate for the people of Madison County, Jackson County, Dekalb County, and the surrounding region. Her areas of practice include Car Wrecks, Personal Injury, DUI and Traffic Tickets, and Premises Liability. Whatever the legal needs, Jennifer’s top priority is always to meet the needs of her clients and make a positive difference in their lives.

Jennifer is married to Nick Pope and she enjoys spending time with her children on Lake Guntersville and attending Alabama Football games. She has a daughter, Bailey, and a son, Lucas.

To set up a FREE in-office consultation with Jennifer Lackey or either of our other attorneys, call (256) 259-3929.
