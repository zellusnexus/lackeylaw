Personal Injury Lawyer FAQ

Following any type of accident, it is important to make sure you are handling the legal aspect of the situation properly. If you have recently received an injury caused by another individual, it is most likely time for you to contact a Huntsville personal injury lawyer.

While this area of law provides great benefit, there is often much confusion surrounding personal injury lawyers. In this post, we will cover some of the most frequently asked questions regarding Huntsville Personal Injury Lawyers. Topics such as what qualifies as a personal injury case, what qualities to look for in a personal injury lawyer, and many others will be discussed.

We hope that by providing this resource, we will offer clarity on how to proceed during this stressful season of your life. You deserve the right to a full recovery. Working with a Huntsville Personal Injury Lawyer is the best way to make that a reality.

What is Personal Injury Law?

Personal Injury Law encompasses any kind of accident or event that was the result of another person’s negligence, carelessness, or wrongdoing. Accidents covered within personal injury law include minor cases such as slip-and-fall incidents as well as serious cases such as wrongful death.

A personal injury lawyer is a professional who has dedicated their life to this specific area of law. By working with a Huntsville personal injury lawyer, clients are financially compensated for the injuries they received as a result of the accident.

While this financial compensation will not reverse the injuries themselves, it will allow the victim to focus on their recovery without the worries of income loss, medical bills, or other related fees.

The most frequently asked question regarding personal injury is whether their unique situation qualifies. While a personal injury lawyer will be able to look at your unique set of circumstances and provide you with a concrete answer, here are just a few examples:

  • Slip & Fall Accident
  • Automobile Accident
  • Wrongful Death
  • Brain Injury
  • Injury During Birth
  • Medical Malpractice
  • Injury or Illness From a Product
  • Injury of Illness From a Medication
  • Other Transportation Accidents (boats, planes, motorcycles, etc.)
  • Workplace Accident
  • Elder Abuse or Neglect
  • Animal Bites

These are just a few of the most common types of injuries that a personal injury lawyer works with on a regular basis. If you have received an injury as the result of another individual’s actions, it is crucial to contact a personal injury lawyer to discuss proper legal action.

Do I Need to Work With a Personal Injury Lawyer?

Although you are able to legally file a personal injury claim without the assistance of a lawyer, it will rarely work out in your favor. Unfortunately, the majority of individuals who represent themself in this type of case rarely receive the compensation they deserve.

Additionally, filing a personal injury claim is a lengthy, strenuous process. As someone who has just experienced a traumatic event and injury, it is important that you allow yourself the mental and physical capability to recover. Taking on the added burden of research, paperwork, and legal meetings will only distract you from your recovery process.

A Huntsville personal injury lawyer will work tirelessly to represent you. Because of this, they will ensure that you receive the financial compensation that you rightfully deserve.

Will I Have to Pay for a Personal Injury Consultation?

Many personal injury lawyers in Huntsville offer complimentary legal advice. The Lackey Law Firm is dedicated to making its legal services available to anyone who is in need. Because of this, they will come to your location if your injuries make it impossible for you to travel.

A free personal injury consultation is the best way to determine how to proceed with your case. During this appointment, an experienced personal injury lawyer will look at the details surrounding your injuries and provide you with a plan going forward.

What Happens If I Don’t Get Money From My Case?

The majority of personal injury lawyers realize that you are contacting them at a very trying time emotionally, physically, and likely financially.

Personal injury lawyers will only agree to work on your case if they believe you have an entitlement to financial compensation. Because of this, you will not pay your personal injury lawyer until you receive compensation for your injuries.

However, what happens if you don’t get money from your case? Each personal injury practice handles this situation differently. However, The Lackey Law Firm believes that their clients should not be required to pay them in the rare occurrence that compensation is not awarded.

What Qualities Should a Personal Injury Lawyer Exhibit?

In most cases, you are working with a personal injury lawyer in one of your most vulnerable seasons. This makes it even more important to hire a personal injury lawyer who is experienced, qualified, and comforting.

Attentive and Comforting

The details surrounding your personal injury case may be of a private nature. For this reason, it is important that you are comfortable discussing these matters with your lawyer. Look for a personal injury lawyer that is attentive and comforting as you communicate.

Clear Communication

Your personal injury case may last several months. One thing that can greatly impact the speed at which a settlement is reached is poor communication. The personal injury lawyer you hire should display clear communication skills and be easy to contact with your questions.

Proven Results

As with anything, you want to make sure that you work with a personal injury lawyer who has proven results. An experienced lawyer will make all the difference in the outcome of your case. Research various personal injury lawyers, reading reviews from past clients. A sure sign of a great personal injury lawyer is the good report they have built with their clientele.

Specializes in Personal Injury Law

Almost any lawyer can represent you in a personal injury case. However, it is important to hire a lawyer who specializes in personal injury law. With extensive experience with this type of situation, a personal injury lawyer will not only provide you with better results but also a less stressful experience.

Do All Personal Injury Cases End in a Trial?

One of the things that inhibit individuals from filing a personal injury claim is that they do not want their case to result in a trial. However, not all personal injury cases end in a trial! In fact, the majority of personal injury cases reach a settlement before the trial in court.

If a settlement is not reached and your case moves on to trial, having an experienced personal injury lawyer on your side will guarantee success.

How Long Do I Have to File a Personal Injury Claim

Each state has a unique set of guidelines and rules regarding personal injury claims. Most states allow you to file a personal injury claim anywhere between two to five years following the accident. It is in your benefit, however, to file a claim immediately following the incident. This helps to ensure that the situation does not drag on longer than necessary.

Finding a Huntsville Personal Injury Lawyer

Personal injury cases are often highly emotional, stressful, and tiresome. For this reason, it is important to hire a Huntsville Personal Injury Lawyer that you fully trust. Finding a Huntsville Personal Injury Lawyer who is experienced and represents each client aggressively is the best way to receive the financial compensation you deserve.
