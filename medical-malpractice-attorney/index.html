Overview

What Is Medical Malpractice?

Medical malpractice occurs when a healthcare provider — doctor, nurse, hospital, or other medical professional — fails to provide treatment that meets the accepted standard of care, and that failure causes injury or death to a patient.


Not every bad outcome is malpractice. Medicine is not guaranteed. But when a provider is negligent and that negligence causes harm, they can and should be held responsible. At The Lackey Law Firm, we have the experience, resources, and medical knowledge to pursue complex malpractice claims against Huntsville-area healthcare providers.

Know Your Rights

Alabama Medical Malpractice Law

The Standard of Care — Proving Malpractice
  • Doctor-Patient Relationship A formal treatment relationship must have existed between you and the provider
  • Breach of Standard of Care The provider did something a reasonable provider wouldn't do — or failed to do what they should have
  • Causation The breach directly caused your injury — not the underlying condition
  • Damages You suffered real harm — physical, emotional, and financial
Expert Witness Requirements

Alabama requires a "certificate of merit" filed with the complaint — a qualified medical expert must certify that:

  • The defendant breached the standard of care
  • The breach directly caused the plaintiff's injuries

We work with respected medical experts from across the country to build strong, well-documented malpractice cases.

Damage Caps

Alabama does not cap economic damages — medical bills and lost wages are fully recoverable. There are potential limits on punitive damages.

Statute of Limitations
  • 2 years from the date of malpractice
  • 6 months from when malpractice was discovered
  • Never more than 4 years (statute of repose)
Exceptions
  • Cases involving minors have different rules
  • Foreign objects left in the body — different discovery timeline
  • Contact us immediately to protect your rights
What You're Up Against

The Challenge of Medical Malpractice Cases

Medical malpractice cases are among the most complex personal injury claims. They require resources, expertise, and a law firm willing to go the distance.

  • Extensive medical knowledge to identify and articulate what went wrong
  • Access to qualified expert witnesses who can testify on your behalf
  • Significant financial resources to fund depositions, experts, and trial
  • Experience with Alabama's specific malpractice procedural requirements
The Other Side Has Unlimited Resources

Insurance companies and hospitals fight malpractice cases aggressively. They have teams of lawyers, in-house medical consultants, and virtually unlimited budgets. You need a law firm with the experience, resources, and willingness to match them — and take the case to trial if necessary.

What You Can Recover

Compensation in Medical Malpractice Cases

Medical Expenses

Past and future treatment, rehabilitation, medications, and long-term care costs.

Lost Income

Wages and salary lost due to inability to work during recovery.

Loss of Earning Capacity

Reduced ability to earn in the future due to permanent injury or disability.

Pain & Suffering

Physical pain and emotional distress caused by the provider's negligence.

Loss of Enjoyment

Inability to participate in activities and the quality of life you had before.

Punitive Damages

Available in cases of gross negligence or intentional misconduct by the provider.

Wrongful Death

When malpractice causes death, surviving family members may recover damages.
