At The Lackey Law Firm, our experienced Huntsville DUI lawyer can help you if you have been charged with driving under the influence (DUI). We understand that a DUI is a serious offense and we are committed to providing our clients with the best legal representation available.

Our Huntsville DUI lawyers are knowledgeable about Alabama’s laws regarding driving under the influence and will provide you with an honest assessment of your case. We will review the facts and identify any possible defenses that may be available to you.

Our lawyers are also familiar with the local courts and prosecutors, which allows us to negotiate effectively on behalf of our clients in order to reach a favorable outcome.
