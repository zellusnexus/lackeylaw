When to Contact a Personal Injury Lawyer for Help

It can sometimes be hard to know when it is appropriate to hire a personal injury lawyer. Many individuals believe that nothing can be done legally in their situation. A personal injury lawyer can assist you in obtaining financial relief. This is true even when something as simple as a slip and fall case is in question.

So, when is it best to work with a Personal Injury Lawyer? Anytime an individual is hurt due to carelessness or negligence of another, a personal injury lawyer should be consulted. Accidents such as the following are covered:

  • slip and falls
  • injury from a defective product
  • sickness from a faulty medication
  • some medical malpractice cases

Additionally, there are many other incidents that are included under personal injury law. A personal injury lawyer can assist you in obtaining financial relief for damages, medical bills, lost wages, and other inconveniences caused by your injury.

A personal injury lawyer should be involved anytime you or a loved one receives injuries due to the carelessness or neglect of another. Even if your injuries were not extensive, a personal injury lawyer can help you obtain financial relief for a loss of income, medical bills, emotional anguish, and other costs or emotional effects of your injury. The list below includes some of the injuries which are typically under personal injury law.

  • Automotive Accidents (Car, Motorcycle, Bus, etc.)
  • Slip and Fall Accidents
  • Some Medical Malpractice
  • Damage Due to Faulty Product
  • Sickness Due to Medication
  • Animal Bites/Attacks
  • Other Injuries Due to Neglect

If you or a loved one has been the victim of one of the accidents listed above, or any other incidents causing an injury, it is important to promptly contact a personal injury lawyer. Working with the personal injury lawyers at The Lackey Law Firm from the onset of your injury is the best way to ensure you are getting the maximum benefit from legal representation.

Benefits of Working With a Personal Injury Lawyer

There are many benefits to working with a personal injury lawyer after experiencing an accident or injury due to the carelessness of another person. In most cases, the individual who caused your injury, even if it was an accident, will have their own legal representation. Obtaining a personal injury lawyer to represent your side of the case will ensure that you receive the restitution you deserve.

Provides Financial and Emotional Relief

There are many other benefits in addition to the financial aspect of working with a personal injury lawyer from The Lackey Law Firm. After an accident or injury, there are many details to discuss. The added stress from these details can cause a much longer recovery time. This has the potential to eventually cost you more money and emotional stress. Some of the damages that they can provide relief from include the following:

  • Medical Bills
  • Loss of Income
  • Emotional Trauma
  • Pain and Suffering
  • Lost Ability to Earn Income 
  • Other Damages Caused by the Accident

Your personal injury lawyer will assess your situation to determine the damages that are a result of your injury or accident. From this consultation, they will begin to develop their case in a way that will be most beneficial to you.

In addition to earning the financial relief you deserve, a personal injury lawyer will also provide mental and emotional relief. In addition to the emotional stress of an injury, medical and legal red tape can become incredibly frustrating. Working with a professional at The Lackey Law Firm is the best way to ensure that you understand every aspect of your personal injury case. This assistance is invaluable.

Offer Objective Insight

Many emotions come to the surface as soon as you or someone you love is the victim of an injury. Fear, frustration, anxiety, anger, and many others can impact your responses to situations. A personal injury lawyer is able to provide you with objective insight into the details of your case. They will assist you in making crucial decisions throughout the duration of your personal injury case. This enables you to receive maximum benefits instead of settling for less than you deserve.

Minimizes Your To-Do List

A personal injury lawyer has many responsibilities throughout the duration of your case. After your consultation, they will begin to investigate the claims. In this process, they begin to establish their case. They will also gather important evidence as needed during this time. 

Your personal injury lawyer is also responsible for negotiating with insurance companies. Negotiating is a task that many people are not familiar with or comfortable with. With this being the case, the negotiation in and of itself provides great value. 

And of course, the final step in the process is to represent you or your loved one in the courtroom trial if a settlement was not agreed upon. This is the primary reason why it is crucial to hire a professional personal injury lawyer as your representation.

Firms with expansive experience such as The Lackey Law Firm are highly skilled in personal injury law. This provides them with the upper-hand once your case enters a courtroom trial. 

Obtains the Restitution You Deserve

When you work with a personal injury lawyer at The Lackey Law Firm, you will easily obtain the restitution you deserve. Although the experts know that this financial restitution will in no way heal you physically, it can provide great relief. In addition to paying for the medical bills that were a result of your injury, this financial restitution can also make up for a loss of work due to the accident.

Financial restitution provides you with the stability to focus on a complete recovery without worrying about the medical bills that you have as a result of the accident.

One of the benefits of working with an experienced personal injury lawyer is that they have the knowledge necessary to calculate the cost of your injury over the span of your life. Because of this, you will get the most out of your personal injury case or lawsuit.
