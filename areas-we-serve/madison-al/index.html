About Our Firm

Fighting for injury victims in every corner of Madison

At The Lackey Law Firm, our team of dedicated Madison personal injury lawyers is committed to protecting the rights of injury victims throughout the City of Madison — from historic Old Madison and the shops along Main Street, to the family neighborhoods of Clift's Cove, Heritage Plantation, and The Reserve. Whether your accident happened along the busy commercial stretch of Madison Boulevard, on the daily commuter corridors of Hughes Road and Wall Triana Highway, near Town Madison and Toyota Field, or out toward Mill Creek and the County Line Road area, we bring decades of local experience to every case. From car accidents and workplace injuries to premises liability, medical malpractice, and wrongful death claims, every injury attorney at our firm understands Alabama law inside and out.

We understand the challenges that follow a serious accident. That’s why our team focuses on recovering all available economic damages, punitive damages, and the mounting medical expenses that pile up after a trip to Madison Hospital or Huntsville Hospital. Wrecks on I-565, Madison Boulevard, Highway 72, and the daily commuter routes connecting Madison to Cummings Research Park and Redstone Arsenal are an unfortunate part of life here — and when an insurance company tries to minimize what you’ve been through, our Madison car accident lawyer team is ready to stand up on your behalf. We are equally honored to support families pursuing a wrongful death claim, offering the steady guidance they need during life’s most difficult moments.

As a trusted Madison personal injury law firm — located just minutes from the city and a short drive from the Madison County Courthouse — we know every case is unique, and we tailor our strategy to the specific needs of each client. Whether you live near Bridge Street and MidCity, in Madison Crossing, Sullivan Farms, or anywhere in between, our firm stands ready to fight for you. At The Lackey Law Firm, we provide comprehensive, aggressive legal representation, ensuring that injury victims throughout Madison have the support they need to move forward. When you choose us, you choose a team that knows personal injury law inside and out — and knows the Madison community like home.
