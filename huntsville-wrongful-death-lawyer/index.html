The Best Wrongful Death Lawyer in Huntsville

Pursuing legal action following the unexpected and wrongful death of a loved one can be an overwhelming process. At The Lackey Law Firm, we understand this and strive to make the process as streamlined and straightforward as possible.

When you allow us to represent you and your family, you can expect to receive trusted legal representation that ensures that your interests are represented during negotiations and at trial.

Benefits of Working With a Huntsville Wrongful Death Lawyer

There are many benefits to working with a Huntsville wrongful death attorney who understands the Alabama civil justice system. In Alabama, there is a two-year statute of limitations on wrongful death claims.

If you have lost a loved one due to someone else’s negligent actions, you may file a lawsuit to receive compensation for your damages within this timeframe. The Lackey Law Firm can help you file your claim quickly and efficiently so that you can begin to heal from this difficult time.

As an experienced Huntsville wrongful death lawyer, we will be able to determine who is responsible for the accident that took your loved one’s life. We understand that this process might be overwhelming, but our team is committed to providing you with the personal attention you deserve.

We will work closely with your family every step of the way to ensure that your case receives the attention it deserves. As a result, we are more likely to obtain a successful verdict for your damages.
